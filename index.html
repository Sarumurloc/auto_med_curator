Understanding Aortic Dissection: A Critical Overview

Consultative Analysis: Is this for you?

Common Anxiety Trigger: Sudden, severe, tearing or ripping pain in the chest, back, or abdomen; unexplained syncope; signs of stroke; severe shortness of breath.

Recommendation: Seek immediate emergency medical attention by calling 911 (or your local emergency number) if you suspect an aortic dissection. Do not attempt to self-diagnose or drive yourself to the hospital.
